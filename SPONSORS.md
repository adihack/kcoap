# Sponsors

kcoap is built in the open and licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Sponsorship keeps it
maintained, tested against real peers and real devices, and reviewed by people outside the project.

## Current sponsors

kcoap has no sponsors yet. The first ones will be listed here.

## How to sponsor

Sponsorship accounts are not set up yet. kcoap will accept sponsorship through
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors) or [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com), and
https://kcoap.dev/sponsors will link the one chosen. Until then, or to talk about sponsorship first,
email Adrian Sołtys at adrian.soltys@seelso.net.

## What funding pays for

- **Interop infrastructure**: CI runners, macOS minutes for the Apple targets, and the containers that
  cross-test kcoap against libcoap and libedhoc.
- **Test hardware**: phones, gateways and boards to run kcoap where it is deployed.
- **Security review**: independent review of the OSCORE and EDHOC code and the crypto backends.
- **Maintainer time**: RFC work, reviews, releases and answering issues.
- **The project's home**: the kcoap.dev domain and website.

How the money is accounted for is in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#sponsorship-money).

## How sponsors are recognized

Sponsors will be listed in this file, in the README's Sponsors section and at
https://kcoap.dev/sponsors. The details, such as logos and their sizes, will be announced together
with the sponsorship accounts.

## Independence

Sponsorship buys no influence. Sponsors get no say over technical decisions, the roadmap, reviews, or
how and when vulnerabilities are disclosed, and no early or private access to vulnerability
information ([governance](GOVERNANCE.md#sponsorship-money)).

## Using the kcoap name as a sponsor

Sponsors may say that they sponsor kcoap. Calling yourself an official partner, or using a kcoap logo,
needs written permission, as [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) describes.
